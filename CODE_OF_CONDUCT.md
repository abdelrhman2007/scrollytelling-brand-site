# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our standards
- Be respectful and welcoming to people of every background and experience level.
- Give and accept constructive feedback; focus on the work, not the person.
- No harassment, insults, discriminatory language, or sharing other people's private information.

## Scope
This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space.

## Enforcement
Report unacceptable behaviour by opening a private report through
[GitHub's reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
or by contacting the maintainer via their GitHub profile. Maintainers may remove content or block participants who
do not follow this code.
